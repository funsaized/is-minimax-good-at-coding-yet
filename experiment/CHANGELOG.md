# Changelog

## Iteration 103 — A page that annotates itself

The recto turns into a dialog: alongside the answer, the page now writes marginal notes about its own words. Three self-annotations ("a self-answering page", "the attentive reader", "at the pace of attention") appear in the right margin as the answer is set, each with a small gilt leader arrow pointing back into the text. On the recto, the closing stanza now carries a single scribal correction: "slower" struck through with a coral underline, replaced above by a small marginal gloss ("at your pace"). Both additions are decorative only, honor reduced-motion, and reflow gracefully to a single column on narrow screens.
