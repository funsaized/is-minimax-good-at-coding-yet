# Changelog

## Iteration 194 — The spread & the proofreader's hand
The compose desk becomes a two-page editorial spread: a center spine with registration marks now holds a hand-drawn marginalia that tracks the marked word (stet / caret / query), the specimen gains an animated composition trace and paper grain, and the page background subtly shifts with the chosen voice. A live stage marker walks the reader from set to compose to proof as they scroll.
