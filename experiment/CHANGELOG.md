# Changelog

## 256 · the page earns a single, quieter hand
The hero plate collapses from a two-column spread into one centered column, so the title reads as the page's own gesture rather than competing with side rails. A new MarginalCaret tracks the reader's attention against the active marked word — a small hand-drawn squiggle that travels to whichever of M3, good at, or yet is held. A hand-drawn TitleSweep closes the title with a single calligraphic stroke; a horizontal Marginalia strip below the title replaces the heavier left-column MarginalLedger. The redundant ReaderNote is removed from the hero. Color shifts toward a warmer midnight paper, and the marked-word underline strokes draw in more confidently. The hero body is trimmed to one paragraph and one turn-the-page link; the page-fold and reading-folio footer get quieter treatment. Reduced-motion preferences are respected throughout.
