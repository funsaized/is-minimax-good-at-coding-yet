# Iteration changelog

## 126 — a small moon pip; a quieted answer plate; a calmer ephemeris.

A hand-inked moon medallion now sits beside the specimen imprint, recording
the moon phase at this reading. The two rotated specimen cards that once
flanked the title are gone — the italic display now reads as one confident
voice. The answer plate's four engraved corners have been replaced with
small gilt dots and slimmer tick rules, so the italic answer becomes the
unambiguous hero of the verso. The ephemeris has lost its overlapping
constellation decoration and its four plate corners, so the almanac and
the volvelle each read as a single composed piece.
