# Changelog

## Iteration 394 — "A Page, Held"
A single italic voice threads the page; the dawn settles; the question holds open.

- A new `ReadingNote` component sets a small italic inscription with a deliberate ornament above and below, a fading caption, and a voice-tinted underline. Three instances appear: beneath the title ("the question, held open — for the reader who arrived in the dark"), at the colophon ("this page is yours — until you ask for another"), and at the foot ("set at first light · read in the dark").
- The dawn above the title is calmer: the sky now holds five deliberate stars (down from twelve), the dashed constellation link is removed, the moon is a single orb with no crescent path, the horizon holds two quiet ticks instead of six, and the orb widens slightly to feel more like one moment of arrival.
- The page inscription below the hero sits in a quieter, voice-tinted frame — a single radial wash and two faint rules that pick up the title's tone, so the pause between the question and the press bed feels held rather than incidental.
- Mobile refinements: reading-note ornaments and rules scale down on small screens, the page inscription's vertical rhythm tightens, and the title's line-height eases so the two-line question never feels cramped at 480px and below.
