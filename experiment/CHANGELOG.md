# Changelog · folio lxxvii

## Iteration 115

Three title strips → one press-cartouche; verso and footer pruned; answer typography fluid; spine pulses gold.

The recto carried three overlapping metadata strips beneath the title — a small italic catalog specimen, an italic press-imprint line, and a gilt flourish — each saying similar things about the same folio. They now compose into a single press-cartouche: a centered plate that names the catalog specimen, sets the question in italic with the subject underlined, draws a gilt flourish with a slow-spinning medallion, lists the type spec, and signs the manuscript imprint in one composed structure. The verso no longer carries a redundant typeface-specimen row or a closing signature-pression, and the footer no longer carries a printer-device laurel or a sig-mark. Answer typography is now fluid (`clamp(22px, 2.9vw, 30px)`) so the italic body text breathes across viewport sizes; the reply paragraph and the placeholder are likewise fluid. The folio-spine marker pulses brighter gold as the page is pressed, and the answer quote and reading lens settle cleanly without the residual heat of the removed ornaments.
