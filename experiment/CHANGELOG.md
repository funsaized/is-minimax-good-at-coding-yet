# Changelog

## Iteration 110 — printer's specimen + press instruction plate + spine thread

Refined the recto into a confident printed broadsheet by binding the question, the marginalia, the read instruction, and the recto seal into one authored composition; added a delicate gold binding thread to the spine.

- Introduced a small italic "Title Specimen" line directly below the question, typeset like a printer's catalog entry (catalog · specimen · no. xviii · set in italic · 30 pt · leaded · with gilt).
- Consolidated the marginalia, the read button, the reader note, and the recto seal into a single bordered "press instruction plate" with corner ticks and double-rule; the marginalia notes become inline marks, the seal becomes a closing line.
- Added a delicate gold "spine thread" to the central binding — a dashed line with nine horizontal stitches and a soft glow that tracks the reading stage.
- Preserved the title drop-cap, edition line, title flourish, and the verso's leaf-turn reveal; the new specimen and plate take the place of three separate ornaments at the foot of the recto.
