## Iteration 238

Hero reads as a letterpress proof: registration marks, a plate stamp, printer-marked words.

The plate stamp sits above the title composition with a single quiet line
("this impression · pulled today"). Four corner registration marks anchor
the spread with subtle L-shapes that pick up the voice tone. Marked words
now default to the same paper ink as the rest of the title, with a colored
printer's underline; they become fully toned only when engaged. The
compose rule draws in more slowly, the publication mark breathes wider,
and the hero plate gains a soft inner frame on wide screens and a tighter
single column on mobile.
