# Iteration 43 — Composed Manuscript

A composed chapter opening and an inked hero emblem.

## What changed

- The chapter's top section — the printer-date, running title
  (capitulum), and rubric ("an inquiry") — is now framed as a single
  authored **Chapter Crown**. Two thin gold rules grow outward from
  the band, with a vermilion and gold pip at the joints between
  elements, so the chapter reads as one composed manuscript band
  rather than three stacked fragments.
- The hero "?" now has an **ink-wash shadow** beneath its main
  stroke — a soft warm halo that draws in alongside the question's
  own quill-trace, like ink that has bled slightly into the
  parchment. A small vermilion **ink-pool** and gold pip pool at the
  foot of the stem, where the question's stroke presses into the
  page. The shadow and pool brighten when the reader acknowledges
  the question, as if the quill pressed harder and a little more ink
  slipped in.
- The redundant "press the mark · the page answers" instruction was
  replaced with a quiet visual cue — a hand-cut gold rule between a
  vermilion and a gold pip, drawn in once the chapter has settled.
- Question typography tightened: line-height nudged to 1.1, a subtle
  text-shadow added for more confidence. The question now reads as a
  single composed phrase rather than a stack of letters.
