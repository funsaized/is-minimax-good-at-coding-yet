## Iteration 302 — composed the front matter into one broadside opening

Composed the title-page header, the spread ribbon, the press rehearsal, the title fold, the reading prologue, and the press lever into a single broadside opening with shared paper grain, a single vertical fold mark, a small masthead plate at the top, and four corner crops. The reader meets one printed sheet on first scroll instead of a stack of strips. Added a small reusable FolioImprint inscription (motto, inscription, sign-off variants) and used it inside the press bed, beneath the colophon, and (via a press motto paragraph) under the press-bed header so the voice repeats itself across the spread without becoming busy. The editor's note was unhooked from the opening sheet and re-anchored so the front matter and the editorial reading breathe separately. Reduced motion honored throughout.
