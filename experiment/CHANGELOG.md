## Iteration 65

Closes the chapter's compositional loop with a scholar's reading seal.

Composes a single authorial mark that closes the chapter where the hero opened it: a small vermilion-and-gold seal set beneath the colophon's "legi · mmxxvi" provenance, bearing a miniature hero "?" inscribed at its heart. Three concentric layers — a soft vermilion press-halo, a gold hairline ring, and an inner vermilion wax field — hold the mark; the miniature question stroke draws itself in pale gold once the seal has pressed into the page. The seal blooms in once the chapter has been fully read (intellexi + annotavi + legi are all in place), with a brief ink-stain pulse: the hero "?" above opened the chapter, the same "?" below now signs it. Adds a faint directional ink-bleed to the answer, reply, and footnote typography so the manuscript passage feels pressed into the parchment rather than placed upon it. Honors reduced-motion: the seal appears in its pressed state without animation, and the ink-bleed offset is unconditional.
