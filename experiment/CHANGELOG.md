## Iteration 219

A quiet refinement pass — the composing room settles into its own atmosphere. Four marks on the same sheet.

- Added a canvas-backed ink-dust layer behind everything: a small, slow drift of particles (acid, coral, blue, paper) that breathes across the page like dust over a working press. Honours `prefers-reduced-motion` and sits behind the grain layer at near-zero cost.
- The hero spread earns four printer's corner registration marks (small circle + crosshairs at each corner) so the page reads as a real sheet pulled from the form, plus a small "set today · folio i" plate element on the top-right. The marks arrive in sequence on first view.
- The hero's title rule now ends with a circled bead instead of a plain dot, with an echo-stroke shadow underneath the wavy line and a second micro-flourish — the closing gesture carries a little more weight.
- The marginalia under the title is wider, has an echo line behind it, and closes with a ringed bead and a small spark on the left — same beat, more deliberate.
- The impression ribbon's "now" label is now a more considered three-cell lockup: a bordered tag, an italic line in the active voice color, and a meta dot+label. It reads like an actual proof note.
- Title typography gains another notch of geometric precision (kerning + ligatures on, slightly tighter letter spacing) and the lead summary uses `text-wrap: pretty` for better ragged edges.
- No new states, no new controls. The page earns the same session in a slightly more atmospheric room.
