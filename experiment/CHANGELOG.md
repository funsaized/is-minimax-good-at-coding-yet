# Changelog

### Iteration 16 — Inscribed Folio
A ribbon, a wax seal, a footnote, and a launch echo refine the folio's closure.

- **Bookmark ribbon.** A slim vermilion fabric ribbon hangs from the top of the page with a forked tail, swaying gently as a real ribbon would. Subtle vertical accent that anchors the manuscript without competing with the chapter.
- **Wax seal at the colophon.** The typeset signature is replaced by a pressed vermilion monogram "Mm" with a wax gradient and a faint inner ring. The signature itself remains alongside it as a small imprint.
- **Manuscript footnote.** A small italic line — "typeset in pixels · lit by attention" — moves from the colophon to a proper footnote below the reply, marked with a roman "i" numeral in vermilion. The colophon becomes lighter and more focused.
- **Bee launch echo.** When the printer's bee lifts off the question mark, a brief bloom of small gold halos fades outward from its origin, giving the flight a sense of weight and lift.
- **Tweaks.** The colophon's signature and folio now sit on a quiet row together; mobile ribbon is hidden below 480 px; reduced-motion fallbacks added for the new components.
