("bestiola") that lives in the bottom-right margin and completes the

default, breathes slowly with a gold belly-glow, parallaxes gently

has been understood (intellexi + annotavi), leaving a trail of nine
small vermilion and gold paw-prints drawn in one slow pass. A small
italic "bestiola" caption sits beneath the creature.
