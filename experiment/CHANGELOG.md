# Changelog

## Iteration 172

Unifies the proof into one quiet folio: a single column, a 3D paper-lift answer, marginalia, and a real-time colophon.

Key changes:
- Unifies the question, answer, and colophon into one reading flow with a left rail holding a Q. mark, thread, and "read slowly" hint.
- Replaces the busy compass with a small coral wax-seal monogram (m³) and a kite ornament in the answer sign-off.
- Replaces the dark "field notes" block with marginalia set in the reading column as italic scribbles with Roman numerals (i., ii., iii.).
- The answer reveal is now a 3D paper-lift: the folded page is rotated on the X axis and lifted up; clicking "turn the page" lets it settle into place.
- Adds a canvas-based dust field of slowly drifting motes for ambient atmosphere, with a soft warm lamp glow in the upper right and a paper grain overlay.
- Adds a real-time colophon ("pressed at HH:MM, [date] · folio clxxii") that reports the local time, a real fact rather than a fabricated stat.
- Refines typography: italic-leaning serif display with tighter letter-spacing, a stronger Q→A rhythm, and a more confident kicker treatment.
- Improves responsive behaviour: the rail collapses to a horizontal cue on small screens, the stamp rotates inline with the body, and the answer columns stack.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`: dust is hidden, the fold animates to its open state immediately, and the hatch is removed.
