# Changelog

## Iteration 104
A recto/verso duet: the reply earns its own incipit, the recto earns its sign-off.

The reply — the page's own voice answering the reader — was previously
set as a single italic line. It now opens with a hand-set editorial header
("the reply · set slowly · in this folio") whose rules extend outward,
typesets in italic at 20 pt, and closes with a dotted-rule colophon
("end of the reply · the page, unchanged"). Below the recto catchword, a
new sign-off marks the recto's end and gestures toward the verso with a
small gold-stroke arrow. The answer surface gains a tiny gold asterism
at the right of its letter-head, which fades in during the press and
shimmers as the answer typesets. The title-subject rule under "Minimax M3"
now reads in a coral-to-gold gradient that fills in on press and breathes
once with a soft gold glow. Mobile layout refined for the new reply
header, closing colophon, and recto sign-off so they read confidently on
small screens. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
