## 400 — a quieter plate, a more composed broadside

- Deepened the night tones and softened the page grain so the dark holds the warm paper more closely.
- Refined the voice palette: `quiet`, `human`, and `bold` are each drawn toward ink-on-plate; the three voices now read more like three sets of the same letter rather than three accents.
- Tightened the hero title: a slightly larger cap, a tighter line stack, and a more confident hairline rule with larger bead markers — the question holds the centre of the plate.
- Brought more warmth and lift to the chase frame around the title: a soft top wash, a matching bottom wash, and a quiet drop shadow that lets the title sit on the page rather than float above it.
- Added a small **fold mark** to every folio turn — a zigzag rule at the top of each section divider that suggests the page turning, mirrored by the existing bottom stitch.
- Introduced a quiet **page breath** between the title inscription and the first folio turn — a thin dashed curve with two outer beads and a central bead that reads as the page exhaling before the press bed.
- Polished the press lever and impression: a softer background wash on the lever, a slightly larger and more confident impression body, and a wider line of type on the pulled sheet.
- Improved mobile behaviour across the hero, the chase frame, the folio turns, and the page breath; reduced the padding and gap stack on small screens while keeping the question and the press bed equally composed.
- Updated FolioTurn so the folio numeral sits between two stitched corner marks instead of one; the section dividers read more like a typeset registration.
