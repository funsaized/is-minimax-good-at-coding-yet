# Changelog

## Iteration 93 — The quiet reading

Recomposed the verso as one reading; the apparatus is now a real index; the folio closes with a colophon and a cat.

### Verso, recomposed
- The answer surface shed its heavy frame; it now reads as paper, not card.
- The reply opens with a printed initial and flows as the next paragraph,
  separated from the answer by a thin rule rather than a new box.
- The redundant corner brackets, quote marks, and attribution were toned down
  or removed.
- Hanging punctuation enabled on the answer copy.
- Wax seal settles into a slightly larger, more deliberate angle once the
  answer is set.

### Apparatus, made navigable
- Every row of the index is now a real link to its section of the folio.
- A small pulsing coral dot marks the currently-visible section as the reader
  scrolls.
- Hash navigation updates the address bar without reloading.
- The redundant closing signature ("manu mea · impressum") moved to the new
  colophon, so the two no longer compete.

### New: Colophon
- A closing signature mark — a gilt aster on a circular plate, beside a
  typeset colophon ("set in · bound at · printed for") and the manu mea sign.
- Arrives after the apparatus, only on complete.

### New: Reader Cat
- A small sleeping cat companion settles at the foot of the verso when the
  reading is complete, with a subtle breath.
- Caption: "quiet reader · settled at the foot of the page".

### Smaller polish
- Title subject colour shifted to a slightly deeper dusk-blue that ties to
  the night sky.
- Question deck trimmed slightly and given a touch more leading.
- Chapter opener padding eased so the question gets more weight.
- Sidebar elements (hour dial, sidereal pocket) sit inside labelled bench
  items so they can be linked from the apparatus.
- Mobile rules extended for the new colophon and reader cat.
