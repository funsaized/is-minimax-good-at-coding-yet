A single dramatic hero. The question mark scaled as a punctuation protagonist; voice specimen as quiet reference.

- **specimen** rows now show the line at two sizes (large display + small reference), with the face set in mono
- **answer** opens with a coloured drop cap "Y" that tracks the voice tone
- **brand mark** refined with an inner hairline and four cardinal dots
- **press seal** and **colophon seal** refined with an inner dashed ring and four cardinal dots
- **question mark** ("yet?") renders its "?" as a scaled punctuation element that grows further when marked
- **hero title** uses display italic with discretionary ligatures and old-style numerals
