# Changelog

## Iteration 191

Folded pressman's note pinned to the left margin — unfolds to a type ladder, your-visit tally, pressed-at time.

## Iteration 190

The press folio's centerpiece becomes a small printshop clock instead of the static press signature. The clock has live hands, a daily phase, and a date window.

## Iteration 189

A small proof sheet, set today — a hand-typeset front matter and a tipped-in answer.

## Iteration 188

The page gains a pressed-paper top edge and a small session map at its foot: title words now carry printer's registration marks when attended.

## Iteration 187

The pencil becomes a proper proofreader's hand: a small palette of standard marks (stet, dele, caret, pilcrow, ?, em-dash) and a crosshair stage to stamp them on.

## Iteration 186

The proof sheet gains a compositor's legend and a print-shop ruler, so the page itself teaches you how to read it.

## Iteration 185

Sharper paper folio: ribbon-tracked reading, paper-slip specimens, cream answer reveal.

## Iteration 184

The three specimen cards become a single typesetter's case with hinged lids, so choosing a voice becomes a physical act.

## Iteration 183

Opened a press drawer: three specimens of the question, each in a different voice; hover one to let the title try it on.
