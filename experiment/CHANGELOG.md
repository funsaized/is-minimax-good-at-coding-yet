# Changelog

## Iteration 63 — Composed the chapter body's inscribed reading title and a scholar's silk margin bookmark

Composed the chapter body's missing headpiece — a small "lectio · the reading" inscription set between two gold rules and a vermilion star-pip — and a slim vermilion silk bookmark hanging in the right margin of the codex frame. Refined the chapter body's typography (larger, more present answer and reply) and tightened the mobile responsive rhythm.

### ReadingTitle — chapter body's inscribed name plate
- Composed a small scholarly inscription at the head of the codex frame, the missing middle term between the folio above (mss · fol. xviii · de initiali lucente) and the colophon below (legi · mmxxvi).
- Two thin gold rules grow outward from a vermilion four-pointed star with a gold pip at its heart; "lectio" set in vermilion italic and "the reading" in ink-soft roman, joined by a hand-cut middot.
- Parallels the colophon plate below at the same scale, so the chapter's terminal headpiece and the body's headpiece pair as marks from the same vocabulary.
- The vermilion star warms subtly when the reader engages the chapter and resolves to a brighter vermilion when the chapter has been fully read.

### MarginBookmark — silk marker in the codex margin
- A slim vermilion silk bookmark hangs in the right margin of the codex frame, like a fabric marker pressed between the folios of an open codex.
- A thin gold rule runs beside it and a small "xviii" monogram rides at its head, so the bookmark reads as belonging to this folio.
- Parallaxes gently with the reader's pointer and sways once the chapter has settled; hidden on reduced-motion devices so the silk appears in its rest state.
- Catches a faint warm glow when the reader leans toward the chapter, like the silk briefly catching candlelight.

### Chapter body typography refinement
- Answer text uses the display serif at clamp(17px, 1.84vw, 24px) with line-height 1.5, so the chapter's opening sentence feels more present and authored.
- Reply text refined to clamp(16.5px, 1.76vw, 22px) with line-height 1.5 and tighter letter-spacing, completing the chapter body's composed reading rhythm.
- Explicit refined to clamp(10.5px, 1.04vw, 13px) with slightly larger spacing, so the chapter's formal closure reads with more quiet weight.

### Mobile responsive tightening
- Refined the 880px, 760px, 620px, and 520px breakpoints so the refined answer, reply, and explicit scale gracefully on tablets and phones.
- The ReadingTitle collapses to a single line with tighter spacing on small screens, and the MarginBookmark tucks closer to the codex edge.

### Polish
- Reduced-motion preferences respected throughout — both new elements hold their rest state when motion is reduced.
- New components honor the existing pointer-engaged and quietus (chapter fully read) states, warming subtly as the reader approaches and the chapter closes.
