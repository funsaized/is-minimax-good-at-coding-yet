# Changelog

## Iteration 8

Refines folio v with a reading-since timestamp, a cursor ink-trail, and a quieter composition.

- Added a faint ink-trail dot that follows the pointer and fades when it rests, so the page quietly marks where attention has been.
- Added a "since HH:MM · Nm read" caption under the corner clock — the bottom-right marginalia now also remembers when you first arrived.
- Tightened the central composition: removed the redundant ruling line, narrowed the vertical gap, and refined the question with the display serif and a hairline accent under "Minimax M3".
- Replaced the static manicule bob with a slower, asymmetrical beckon; on hover or focus of the question mark it leans in more urgently.
- Recast the colophon lines as a single attributions couplet plus a quieter second line.
- Cooled the dust palette with a higher share of moonlit particles, and reduced the watermark's resting opacity so the auriole owns the center.
- Improved responsive behaviour: the "since" marker scales down with the rest of the marginalia; the ink-trail halo tightens on small screens.
- Title preserved: "is Minimax M3 good at frontend yet?"
