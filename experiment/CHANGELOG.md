# Changelog

## Iteration 413 · the press now feels like one moment

- The press bed becomes a single authored event: a thin press-arm now visibly
  carries force from the lever to the impression, pulsing across the table on
  each pull.
- A voice-tinted ink-wash ripples across the whole press table on every pull,
  so the entire press region feels the moment instead of just the local cells.
- The lever now settles with a tactile four-keyframe bounce (knob overshoot,
  rebound, settle) and the cage briefly glows in the active voice color.
- The impression gains a stronger stamp header (italic display number +
  voice letter set in serif italic, with a quiet caption) and a new ink
  landing line that draws under the body and dissolves, making the
  impression feel like it was just pressed onto paper.
- Marked words in the impression now hold a faint ink shadow, sharpening
  the contact between the line and the page.
- A press-bed base plane runs under both cells, pulsing on pull, so the
  lever and impression read as parts of one machine.
- Removed a small amount of dead CSS from a previous iteration.

Everything else is preserved — title, dawn, composing case, hero, motto,
reading cord, proof line, notation key, answer, colophon, dusk, and the
held question all stand as they were.
