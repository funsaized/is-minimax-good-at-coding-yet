# Changelog

## 370 — the question mark as author
A single dramatic hero plate; the title now sets the question across two lines, with the question mark arriving as the climax of the second. Corner ornaments retired in favour of one printer's flourish above the title. The answer unfolds like a folded sheet, the press leaves an ink-bleed when pulled, and the colophon signs off with a wax seal and a quiet signature line.

- Two-line hero title; the integrated question mark scales to 2x and arrives with its own spring when "yet" is marked.
- New `PrinterFlourish` SVG used as the opening mark, the hero's quiet flourish above the title, and the colophon's signature line.
- New `PrinterMark` replaces the topbar's hand-drawn brand glyph; rotates gently on hover.
- Answer card now uses a paper-fold transition (`scaleY` + soft shadow at the crease); renamed `answer__fold` → `answer__crease`.
- Press bed adds an `ink-bleed` flash around the knob and a softer ink-bleed into the impression when pulled, plus a session pull tally and a thin progress rail on the lever.
- Marginalia pins (tack, clip, pin) gain inner detail; slip rotations softened.
- Specimen plates drop the duplicate small sample; a small italic caption line sits below the big sample.
- Colophon seal gains a radial glow, a tone-tinted drop shadow, and a printer's flourish above the sign-off.
- Topbar gains a small live clock next to the date (hidden on small screens).
- Mobile hero spacing tightened; reduced min font-size for the two-line title.
