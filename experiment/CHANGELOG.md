# Iteration 18

A careful chapter — the question itself, set with care.

## Summary

Removed live-status chrome, tightened the chapter composition, strengthened the
question's typography as the chapter's true centerpiece, and added a rubricated
drop-cap initial to open the answer.

## Removed

- **Cursor ink-trail** — a dot under the pointer broke the manuscript fiction;
  a folio does not leak ink where the reader looks.
- **Clock + "reading since" + elapsed minutes** — these were dashboard widgets
  in a poem; the surrounding viewer owns time, the folio owns the question.
- **Hover gloss line** — competed with the answer for the eye; the answer
  is the only thing the chapter has to say back.
- **Manicule pointing glyph** — a redundant invitation; the question mark
  already invites.
- **Footnote line** — "typeset in pixels · lit by attention" was a tagline
  sitting where the answer should land. The colophon already closes the
  folio.
- **Unused `Asterism` ornament** — dead weight.

## Added

- **Rubricated drop-cap initial** — a small painted vermilion "I" that opens
  the answer line, set as a manuscript illuminated initial with a vermilion
  scoring line beneath. Pulls the eye into the chapter's reply as the
  climax lands.
- **Bee's carried parchment** — when the Aldine bee arrives at the margin
  it unfurls a tiny rolled vellum beneath it. The bee is now a courier
  bearing a written reply to the corner, not just a hovering mark.

## Strengthened

- **Question typography as the chapter's centerpiece** — the question
  text now reads as the chapter title, not a subtitle. Clear hierarchy:
  "is" (small ink-soft), "Minimax M3" (gold small-caps), "good at
  frontend" (ink), "yet?" (gold-bright, larger, weightier, with a
  stronger text-glow). The climax earns its landing.
- **Composition rhythm** — removed competing vertical elements so the
  chapter breathes; the question and its answer sit cleanly together.
- **Marginalia** — kept the two quiet corners (top-left folio note,
  bottom-right "now"), renumbered to folio xviii.
- **Capitulum, rubric, incipit, fleuron, brackets, marginal rubric,
  illuminated star, auriole, watermark, ribbon, taper, paper grain,
  bifolio, colophon, wax seal, signature, catchword** — all preserved
  and retuned for iteration xviii.

## Notes

- The hero's gaze-tracking parallax and the canvas embers are retained.
- All decorative motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile, small-mobile, and tiny-screen breakpoints re-tuned for the
  shorter chapter piece.
- No fabricated metrics, status, or iteration counts introduced; the
  folio number (xviii) is the only ordinal, and it is part of the
  ongoing colophon convention.
