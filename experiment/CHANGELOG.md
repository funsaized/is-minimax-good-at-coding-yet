# Changelog

## Iteration 112

A composed celestial volvelle unites the verso's ephemera, the chapter mark is set with more authority, and the reply now opens with a confident italic drop-cap.

- Composed the three separate bench dials (hour, sky, moon) into a single celestial volvelle that reads hour, sky, and moon phase on concentric rings of one instrument, with a printed caption and hour/moon/sky readouts.
- Refined the chapter mark: the Roman numeral is set larger and typeset with a fine gold rule beneath it, giving the chapter heading a more confident printed feel.
- Added the year in Roman numerals to the chapter-witness inscription, alongside day, ordinal date, month, and time.
- Replaced the small reply drop-cap with a VersoDropCap: a tall gilt-framed italic initial with vine ornaments in the corners and a fine gold baseline rule, echoing the recto's broadsheet initial.
- Updated the constellation line above the bench into a soft separator that lets the volvelle be the center of the ephemeris plate.
- All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; the volvelle's hands, sky rotation, and Polaris pulse stop when the user prefers reduced motion.
