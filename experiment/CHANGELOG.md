# Changelog

## 114
Recto meta-pair → one italic press-imprint. Verso gains a margin rule, recto a leaf-shadow, answer plate is quieted.

The title now sits above one italic press-imprint line that gathers the impression, the chapter, the folio and the motto — `first impression · manus m · iii · caput xviii · ad lucem · in folio lxxvii`. The verso's inside margin carries a thin ochre rule with three ticks (the answer, the reply, cap · xviii) that draws in when the leaf is revealed. When the verso opens, a warm leaf-shadow pools along the recto's right inner edge. The answer plate's rim, title-block and corners are dampened so the illuminated initial and italic answer read as the verso's hero.
