# Changelog

## Iteration 135 · the page speaks for itself
The recto earns its quiet by replacing three stacked sub-widgets
(folio-ledger, reading-tally, specimen-imprint) with a single composed
almanac band of today · hour · moon · readings. The title loses its
verb-pivot and flourish-trail so the italic reads as one continuous
gesture; a quiet paper-fold anchor closes the title block. The answer
and reply lose their competing drop caps, so the broadsheet drop cap
on the question remains the singular ornament of the folio. The
Apparatus index is dropped — the ReaderTide already serves as the
folio's nav. Mobile guards keep the title legible and the question
panel uncrowded on small screens.
