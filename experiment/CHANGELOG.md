# changelog

## iteration 382 — the question, set with room around it
the chase loses its measurement column and the dawn becomes a single crescent; a compositor's note reads below the line.

- the chase frame around the question is lightened further — outer rule a touch thinner, corner quoins shrunk to 14px, inner rule softened, so the title feels set, not held.
- the type-bed column on the chase's left edge, the type-high guide on its right edge, and the bottom-right quoin stamp are removed — they were craft theatre that competed with the line. the chase now reads as a single, breathing room.
- the dawn scene is replaced with a single crescent moon cradling the title — a lit disc with a dark bite in the upper right, set above a thin dashed horizon with four quiet typesetter's ticks. the rays, motes, drifting stars, and horizon scene are removed; one moon carries the meaning.
- a compositor's note reads below the title — a small italic line, set in two columns with a printer's fleuron mark on the left. the note changes with the active voice: "set the line softly, that the reader may hear themselves in it" (quiet), "set the line by hand, that the page may feel less like a page" (human), "set the line at full height, that the question may be heard once and clearly" (bold). the fleuron mark also sits in the colophon as the page's signing-off mark.
- when the lever is pulled, the chase takes a quiet breath — a 0.9s ease-out scale pulse that makes the page feel like it just received the impression. the compositor's fleuron mark brightens briefly at the same moment.
- mobile layout for the compositor note collapses to a single column at 540px, with the fleuron mark shrinking to 32px wide.
- on prefers-reduced-motion, the new chase-breath animation and compositor-mark pulse are disabled.
