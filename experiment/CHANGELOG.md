# Changelog

## Iteration 49 — A scholar's oculus; tighter composition rhythm; more readable chapter body

A small hand-drawn eye motif in the colophon completes the almanac pair at the top of the composition (horologium · speculum · oculus — time, the heavens, and the observer). The composition gains a touch more breathing room between the hero and the question, and the chapter body (answer, reply) is set in slightly larger, more legible type so the chapter reads as one deliberate gesture rather than a tightly packed column.
