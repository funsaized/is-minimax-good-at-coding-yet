# Changelog

## Iteration 66 — one scholarly hand

Composed the chapter as one deliberate page: parchment over card, terser marginalium, a reader's pencil under the verb.

- **Question-block as parchment.** Retired the iteration-21 heavy backdrop (layered radial gradients, inset gold border, dark drop shadow) and the iteration-44 "card-lifted-from-the-desk" press-mark in favour of a single warm halo behind the chapter. The chapter sits inside the folio now, not above it.
- **Composed body, fewer marks.** Suppressed the redundant printer's ornament that was sitting between the answer and the reply; the reading-guide now owns the chapter's mid-paragraph breath on its own. Slimmed the tail-piece so it reads as one composed close with the explicit's pilcrow.
- **Body typography.** Lifted the answer and reply into a cleaner scholarly voice — slightly larger italic, more breathing in line-height, and softer text-shadow so the prose reads as ink against the candle instead of against competing glow.
- **Reader's pencil on the predicate.** The gold rule beneath "good at frontend" now carries a small vermilion tap at its right edge in steady state, and on a reader's `is-pointing` the rule crosses from gold to vermilion while the tap extends a hair brighter — the verb has just been underlined.
- **Tighter marginalium.** The three corner whispers (a folio, the reading, now) shrunk to single short lines — "a folio, slowly composed", "candle · reader · page — slow down", "the page will wait for you" — the way a careful reader would scribble in the margin of a borrowed codex.
- **Mobile composition.** Tightened answer and reply type at narrow widths, and shrunk the predicate-rule tap to keep it readable on the smallest screens.

Reduced-motion behaviour is preserved throughout: the hidden ornaments stay hidden on touch devices, the predicate-rule transitions shorten to 0.2s opacity crossfades, and the new warm pool fades rather than transforms.
